How to play

One raid lasts 7 nights. Every night Squeektoshi really runs on the WHEELY9000 — that night's distance and blocks are your party's strength, and the hours he snoozes are the boss's counter-attack. Each fighter's damage = ATK × km/10 × card multiplier. Kill the boss before it counters and the party takes no damage. The boss has 3 phases: the weaker it gets, the angrier — and in phase 3 it heals 300 HP on any night with ≥ 19 snooze hours. Slay the Snooze King (6,400 HP) within 7 nights to win; run out of nights or party HP and you lose.

Night data comes straight from api.squeekchain.com (the 6 most recent complete nights); night 7 is the “Decision Night”, simulated from the weekly average and labelled as such. Real recorded data: the hamster runs 7.5–11.7 km a night and snoozes ~19 hours a night.

Wallet & Sepolia: connect a wallet (top right) — the game switches it to the Sepolia testnet and shows your Squeeks, power and claimable SQK from the Squeekchain API. Win a raid and you can claim the victory on-chain: a real Sepolia transaction in test ETH (0 ETH to yourself, with the raid result encoded in the transaction data — verifiable by anyone on Etherscan). No wallet? The raid plays exactly the same. Need test ETH? Grab some from a Sepolia faucet.
